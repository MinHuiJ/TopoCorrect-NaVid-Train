Auxiliary loss and gradient tests

TopoCorrectStateModule.compute_topocorrect_aux_losses accepts optional landmark,
pointer, progress, failure, correction, recovery-mode, and recovery-target labels.
No labels returns (None, {}), leaving LM behavior unchanged. Labels are loss targets
only and never enter inference encoders.

The readiness test verifies finite nonzero gradients for pointer key, failure,
correction, recovery mode, and recovery-target projections. NST tests cover
instruction projector, landmark head, nav query, cross attention, fusion, and delta
projection gradients.
