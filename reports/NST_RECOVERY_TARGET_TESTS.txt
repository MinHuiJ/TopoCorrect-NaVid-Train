NST recovery-target tests

NavigationStateEncoder computes recovery_target_logits by projected nav-state to
projected TST-node similarity (topocorrect_navid/model/state_tokens.py:285-288).
Shape is [B,N]; TST padding nodes are masked to negative infinity. Recovery mode
head shape is [B,4] with fixed indices CONTINUE, BACKTRACK, REORIENT, STOP_RECHECK.

The 23-test command records [2,2] target logits for two nodes and [1,1] for a
single-node TST. The recovery projection receives gradients. This is prediction-only:
no backtrack, action override, Agent change, or actual recovery execution exists.
