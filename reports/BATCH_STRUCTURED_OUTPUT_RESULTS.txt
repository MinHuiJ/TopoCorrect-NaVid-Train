Padding rules
- node state/revisit padding is zero; node_mask padding is False; original node
  and time identities padding is -1.
- instruction-related numerical padding is zero and instruction_mask padding is
  False.
- recovery_target_logits invalid/padded node positions are -inf. The NST encoder
  rejects all-masked topology input before any topology softmax/attention use.
